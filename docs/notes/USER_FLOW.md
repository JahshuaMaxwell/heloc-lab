# User flow

1. Open the dashboard. The workshop example is loaded: a $150,000 HELOC, $75,000 outstanding, sample pay and bills, and four other debts. The banner states that the model is educational and that home-secured debt can lead to foreclosure.
2. Read the metric cards and the narrative under them. Olive interest bars met every modeled payment. Terracotta bars did not. Do not treat the lowest interest number as the recommendation when its card says payments were missed.
3. Open **HELOC** and replace the sample limit, balance, rates, dates, term, and fees with the household’s figures. The side panel updates utilization, available credit, and the interest estimates.
4. Open **Cash flow**. Add each paycheck with its real frequency and next pay date. Enter living expenses on the dates they are paid. Leave credit-card and loan payments off this list if those debts are entered on the consolidation screen. Set the emergency reserve. Leave “use the emergency fund” off if the workshop should show shortfalls instead of spending savings.
5. Open **Strategies**. Compare minimum payments, a $2,000 total payment, a $2,000 additional principal payment, the entire paycheck, and 25%, 50%, and 75% paycheck shares. Select the path to feature on the dashboard. Add another percentage if the household wants a different split.
6. Open **Consolidation**. Enter each other debt, mark which balances would move to the HELOC, and compare the four plans. Confirm the “after transfer” household debt is not lower than the debt before the transfer.
7. Open **Stress test**. Review unchanged rates, increases of 1, 2, and 3 points, a decrease, an immediate end to the promotion, and a custom schedule if one was entered.
8. Open **Scenarios** to name and save the case, duplicate it for a variation, and compare up to three saved cases. Saved cases remain in this browser.
9. Open **Learn** when a participant asks what a HELOC, daily interest, or velocity banking means, or when the foreclosure and consolidation warnings need to be read aloud.
10. Open **Report**, review the on-screen packet, and download the PDF for the workshop file.

Use **Reload workshop example** to return to the sample case.
